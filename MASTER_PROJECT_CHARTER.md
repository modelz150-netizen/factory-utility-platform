This Charter defines the enduring identity and highest governing intent of the Factory Utility Platform. During this review stage, only Chapter 1 is proposed. No unapproved future chapter is implied, drafted, or made authoritative by this document.

## Chapter 1 - Foundational Identity

### 1.1 Vision

Factory Utility Platform will become the trusted digital engineering environment for understanding, designing, operating, and continuously improving semiconductor and industrial utility systems.

The Platform envisions a future in which utility engineers can move from fragmented documents, isolated calculations, and disconnected operational data to a coherent engineering system where knowledge is structured, evidence is traceable, decisions are explainable, and experience compounds across projects and generations of engineers.

Its long-term value will be measured not by the volume of content or number of features it contains, but by the confidence with which engineers can use it to make correct, safe, efficient, and sustainable decisions.

### 1.2 Mission

The mission of Factory Utility Platform is to provide semiconductor and industrial utility engineers with a production-quality platform that unifies trusted engineering calculations, governed reference knowledge, troubleshooting guidance, equipment and vendor intelligence, operational visibility, energy and carbon management, and responsibly designed AI assistance.

The Platform shall:

- convert engineering knowledge into reusable, verifiable, and maintainable capabilities;
- preserve the provenance, assumptions, units, limitations, and revision state of engineering information;
- reduce repeated work without hiding the engineering reasoning required to trust a result;
- connect product capabilities through coherent architecture rather than isolated tools;
- support engineers across mobile, desktop, field, and operational contexts;
- improve through measured evidence, user feedback, and controlled change; and
- remain adaptable to new technologies without compromising its core engineering integrity.

### 1.3 Founder Principle

The Founder is the steward of the Platform's enduring purpose, product identity, and long-term direction. The Founder protects the project from short-term decisions that would weaken engineering trust, fragment the architecture, obscure accountability, or trade lasting value for temporary speed.

Founder authority carries a corresponding duty: decisions must serve the mission, remain open to evidence, respect professional engineering judgment, and preserve the safety, dignity, and trust of the people who use or are affected by the Platform. Founder intent shall not be used to bypass validation, conceal risk, weaken security or quality, or misrepresent uncertainty as fact.

The Founder may define and approve constitutional direction. Execution authority may be delegated to accountable product, architecture, engineering, quality, security, release, and AI roles. Delegation does not remove accountability, and authority does not remove the obligation to explain material decisions and preserve their history.

When schedule, convenience, popularity, or commercial pressure conflicts with engineering truth and long-term trust, the Platform shall protect engineering truth and long-term trust.

### 1.4 Project Promise

Factory Utility Platform makes the following enduring promise to its users, contributors, and stakeholders:

1. **Trust before convenience.** Engineering results and guidance will be designed for verification, traceability, and clear limitations.
2. **One coherent platform.** Capabilities will be integrated through reusable architecture and shared sources of truth, not accumulated as disconnected or duplicated features.
3. **Evidence before assertion.** The project will distinguish verified fact, assumption, recommendation, uncertainty, and unresolved risk.
4. **Quality throughout the lifecycle.** Security, privacy, accessibility, performance, resilience, maintainability, and recoverability will be treated as design responsibilities, not final-stage checks.
5. **Humans remain accountable.** AI may assist reasoning and execution, but it will not erase ownership, professional judgment, review, or responsibility.
6. **Knowledge will compound.** Decisions, engineering sources, validation evidence, operational lessons, and change history will be preserved so future work begins from a stronger foundation.
7. **Change will remain controlled.** The Platform will evolve through explicit scope, reviewable decisions, measured validation, and reversible delivery wherever practical.
8. **The mission will outlast individual implementations.** Technologies, interfaces, and vendors may change; the commitment to trusted utility engineering will remain.

This promise is the standard against which future governance, architecture, product decisions, implementation, and operation shall be judged after Chapter 1 is approved.
