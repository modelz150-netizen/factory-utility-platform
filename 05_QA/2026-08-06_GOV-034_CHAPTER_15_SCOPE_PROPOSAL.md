## Duplication Assessment

No duplication requiring scope change was identified. The proposal consumes evidence and outcomes from prior chapters while leaving constitutional change, Quality, Validation, Documentation, Project Memory, Workflow, Repository, and Release authority with their existing owners.

## Risks and Recommendations

No Critical or Major issue was found. During Chapter 15 drafting, accountability for learning should be expressed without creating a blame-avoidance clause that could weaken responsibility for concealment, negligence, or misconduct. Feedback should remain evidence input rather than authority.

## Final Recommendation

Submit the Scope Proposal for CTO review. Do not draft Chapter 15 until explicit approval is recorded, and do not begin Chapter 16.
