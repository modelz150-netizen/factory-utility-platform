### Minor Issues

1. Permanent succession designation requires a future approved constitutional record and cannot be completed by Chapter text alone.
2. Detailed responsibility matrices remain lower-level work and must not become competing sources of constitutional authority.
3. Charter amendment mechanics remain pending under `GOV-008` and are correctly excluded.

### Risks

- Delegated mandates may overlap if future operational role records do not identify a single accountable approving authority.
- Emergency governance could become normalized without time limits and retrospective review in lower-level procedures.
- Repository or commercial control could be mistaken for constitutional authority unless succession records preserve Chapter 8 distinctions.

### Recommendations

1. Create reusable decision-right and temporary-delegation records under the existing Governance templates when operational implementation begins.
2. Establish a separately approved constitutional succession record consistent with Chapter 8.
3. Preserve `GOV-008`, `GOV-010`, `GOV-011`, and `GOV-016` as future Governance work.
4. Do not begin Chapter 9 until Chapter 8 is explicitly approved and PR #9 is merged.

## Final CTO Scope Validation

The CTO approved Chapter 8 subject to a final scope boundary: the chapter may define Governance structure, constitutional stewardship, Founder responsibilities, delegated authority, decision accountability, conflict resolution, temporary delegation, and Founder succession, but not operational Governance processes.

Review identified one scope defect: the draft contained a separate `Emergency Governance` section that could be interpreted as an operational Governance process outside the approved Chapter 8 responsibility. The section was removed. Emergency continuity remains addressed only where necessary within temporary delegation and incapacity, without prescribing an operating workflow.

The Governance records section remains because it defines constitutional decision accountability and authority traceability rather than a documentation workflow. It does not prescribe tools, templates, routing, meetings, or operational procedures.
