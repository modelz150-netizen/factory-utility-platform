## Context

Describe the forces, constraints, evidence, and problem requiring a durable decision.

## Decision

State the decision precisely, including boundaries and effective conditions.

## Alternatives Considered

Summarize viable alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, risks, operational obligations, migration impact, and reversibility.

## Validation

Define how the decision will be verified and what evidence is required.

## Supersession

Link any decision this ADR supersedes or the later ADR that supersedes it.
